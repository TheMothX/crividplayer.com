What we just shipped, and what is next

Crivid is a one-time purchase, so the fair question is whether it keeps getting better. This page is the answer.

Shipped

The last few weeks, newest first.

  • 8 Sep
    Apple TV update is liveThe guide now keeps working from your saved copy when a provider answers with something unexpected.
  • 7 Sep
    Press and hold up now works everywhereRolling out to Android now. It used to stop responding after the first jump.
  • 4 Sep
    The guide keeps working when your provider misbehavesA bad reply no longer wipes the copy you already had.
  • 3 Sep
    Search moved into the top barLive, Movies and Series. Hold a result to pin or hide it.
  • 3 Sep
    Apple TV caught up with AndroidThe whole feature list, not a subset.
  • 2 Sep
    The guide opens in about a secondIt was closer to nine. Same data, kept in a faster shape.
  • 2 Sep
    Parental controls across the whole appA PIN on movies and series too, not just live TV, and a word filter so locked things stay out of search.
  • 1 Sep
    Lock and hide, per playlistDifferent rules for different sources.

Working on

Decided and underway. No dates, because dates in software are guesses.

  • RecordingDecided and mapped out. It records while the app is open. Recording with the app closed is not promised.
  • Download to watch offlineMovies and series saved to the device, so a train ride or a cabin without signal is not a problem. Comes after recording, since the two share the same foundation.
  • Amazon AppstoreBuilt and ready. Waiting on the store side.
  • Microsoft StoreThe Windows build exists. The listing is in review.
  • Breadbin BlueA Commodore 64 theme. Design approved, not built yet.

Not planned

Worth knowing before you buy.

  • ChromecastNot there today. We would rather say so than have you find out after paying.
  • AirPlay videoAirPlay carries audio only right now.
  • Channels of our ownNever. Crivid is a player. You bring your own provider, and that will not change.
