What we just shipped, and what is next

Crivid is a one-time purchase, so the fair question is whether it keeps getting better. This page is the answer.

Shipped

The last few weeks, newest first.

  • 25 Sep
    Apple TV 1.9.20 is liveA new engine for Live TV that starts faster and comes back on its own. Radio channels show what is playing. Breadbin Blue theme.
  • 24 Sep
    iPhone, iPad and Mac 1.9.1 is liveWhen a live channel stops, the app keeps trying and tells you why. Movies and series resume where you left off. Plex, Jellyfin and Emby libraries, and twelve themes with Breadbin Blue. Download to watch offline on iPhone and iPad.
  • 23 Sep
    Android 1.9.5 is in review at Google PlayTwelve themes, Breadbin Blue included, and categories shown as cards. Download movies and whole seasons to watch offline. The guide opens faster and shows time left.
  • 19 Sep
    Apple TV search got its own letter rowThe keyboard no longer covers your results, and matches show up as you type.
  • 8 Sep
    Apple TV update is liveThe guide now keeps working from your saved copy when a provider answers with something unexpected.
  • 7 Sep
    Press and hold up now works everywhereIt used to stop responding after the first jump.

Working on

Decided and underway. No dates, because dates in software are guesses.

  • Radio and music channelsThe channel logo, what is playing and the clock instead of a black screen. In testing on Android TV. Apple TV has it, iPhone and Mac are next.
  • Remote fixes on Android TVUP and DOWN from pause, and UP from the guide to search. In testing.
  • Amazon AppstoreBuilt and ready. Waiting on the store side.
  • Microsoft StoreThe Windows build exists. The listing is in review.

Not planned

Worth knowing before you buy.

  • ChromecastNot there today. We would rather say so than have you find out after paying.
  • AirPlay videoAirPlay carries audio only right now.
  • Channels of our ownNever. Crivid is a player. You bring your own provider, and that will not change.
