What the price is, exactly

$17.99 is the United States price. Other countries are priced for their own region and shown in your local currency — in Norway, for example, it is kr 219 including VAT. Where local sales tax or VAT is added at checkout, you will see it before you confirm, and the amount shown there is always the amount you pay.

Pro is a one-time purchase for the lifetime of the app on the platform you bought it on. There is no recurring charge, no upsell inside the app, and no advertising.

Where you can buy it

Pro is sold through Google Play and the App Store. Whichever you buy through handles the payment and issues the receipt. Versions for other platforms are in progress; each will state its own purchase method on this page when it arrives.

Pro is tied to the store you bought it from. Buying on Android does not unlock Pro on an iPhone or iPad, or the other way round — the two stores cannot see each other's purchases.

What you are paying for — and what you are not

Crivid Player is a media player. It includes no channels, films or streams of any kind. Pro unlocks features in the software; it does not include, provide or grant access to any content. To watch anything you supply your own source — an M3U playlist, an Xtream login, a network tuner, or your own media server — and you are responsible for holding the necessary rights or subscriptions for it.
