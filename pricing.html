What the price is, exactly

kr 219 is the Norwegian price, including VAT. If you buy in another country you will see the price in your own currency, set for your region and including any local tax. The amount shown at checkout before you confirm is always the amount you pay.

Pro is a one-time purchase for the lifetime of the app on the platform you bought it on. There is no recurring charge, no upsell inside the app, and no advertising.

Where you can buy it

Pro is currently sold through Google Play, which handles the payment and the receipt. Versions for other platforms are in progress; each will state its own purchase method on this page when it arrives.

What you are paying for — and what you are not

Crivid Player is a media player. It includes no channels, films or streams of any kind. Pro unlocks features in the software; it does not include, provide or grant access to any content. To watch anything you supply your own source — an M3U playlist, an Xtream login, a network tuner, or your own media server — and you are responsible for holding the necessary rights or subscriptions for it.
